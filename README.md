# Employee Attrition & HR Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition using Python and data analysis techniques.

The goal is to understand employee turnover and identify factors associated with employee attrition.

## Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

- Employees: 1,470
- Columns: 35
- Target variable: `Attrition`
- `Yes` = Employee left the company
- `No` = Employee stayed with the company

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

The project analyzes employee attrition based on:

- Overall attrition
- Department
- Job Role
- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Age
- Monthly Income
- Job Level
- Years at Company
- Distance From Home
- Years Since Last Promotion

## Key Insights

- Employees working overtime show higher attrition compared with employees who do not work overtime.
- Attrition rates vary across different job roles.
- Different job levels show different attrition patterns.
- Job satisfaction and work-life balance are associated with differences in attrition.
- Employees who leave the company differ from retained employees in factors such as age, income, experience, and distance from home.
